# 0.5.1 — 2026-08-30

## Changed

- **Missing observations** — `bls_get_series` and `bls_get_latest` expose `available`, distinguish total rows from published values, and render the BLS `-` sentinel as unavailable; DataCanvas rows add nullable `value_numeric`. ([#58](https://github.com/cyanheads/bls-labor-mcp-server/issues/58))
- **Mixed-series batches** — valid observations remain available when another SeriesID is invalid or has no data, with reason-specific guidance for each unresolved ID. ([#59](https://github.com/cyanheads/bls-labor-mcp-server/issues/59))
- **Recovery guidance** — service-raised quota, lock, unsupported-calculation, availability, and serialization failures include their declared recovery hints in structured and text responses. ([#60](https://github.com/cyanheads/bls-labor-mcp-server/issues/60))

## Fixed

- **Invalid Series advisories** — malformed SeriesIDs surface as typed not-found failures or `bls_get_latest.failed[]` entries instead of empty successful results. ([#61](https://github.com/cyanheads/bls-labor-mcp-server/issues/61))
